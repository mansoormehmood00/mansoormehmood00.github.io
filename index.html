Approach

Two cycles, run end to end

The sales cycle that brings practices in, and the people cycle that builds the team to serve them. I own both, and each one is built around what the client or the hire actually needs.

  1. 01

    Prospecting

    Choose target specialties and states, build prospect lists, and run outreach by phone, email and LinkedIn, reworking scripts based on what actually converts.

  2. 02

    Qualification

    Qualify every lead in HubSpot CRM and set clear follow-up schedules and weekly pipeline targets for the team.

  3. 03

    Discovery

    Find the problem the practice actually has, such as claim denials, slow reimbursements or credentialing delays, instead of pitching a fixed package.

  4. 04

    Proposal & pricing

    Price each proposal around the practice’s claim volume, payer mix and denial history, and track competitor pricing to sharpen offers and messaging.

  5. 05

    Negotiation & contract

    Handle presentations, objections and pricing negotiation through to a signed contract.

  6. 06

    Handover & retention

    Hand new clients to the billing and credentialing teams, stay the main contact for key accounts, keep retention high with regular check-ins, and spot add-on service opportunities.

  1. 01

    Define the role

    Write clear job descriptions for the business development team, built around what the role really has to deliver.

  2. 02

    Source & shortlist

    Source candidates through channels such as LinkedIn Recruiter, Rozee.pk and Indeed, and shortlist the strongest fits.

  3. 03

    Interview & select

    Interview candidates and recommend final selections to senior management.

  4. 04

    Onboard & train

    Train new hires on the sales process, HubSpot CRM, company policies, and the basics of US medical billing and insurance.

  5. 05

    Coach & measure

    Set individual KPIs and coach reps through call reviews and one-on-one feedback, with daily activity targets and weekly pipeline reviews.

  6. 06

    Appraise & support

    Run performance appraisals, manage attendance, leave and shift schedules, and act as the first point of contact for employee concerns and HR policy questions.
